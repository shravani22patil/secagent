How to read results

Version-matched

The detected product and version fall inside a vulnerable range in the local CVE knowledge base. It is a lead to verify, not a proven exploit.

Not retested

SecAgent does not re-scan after fixes. Each remediation step shows retest status honestly until you re-run the assessment.

Mock data

In mock mode, results come from built-in fixtures. No packets are sent to the target.
